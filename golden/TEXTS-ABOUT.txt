The golden test texts - where they come from
============================================

texts/own_*.txt (8 Hebrew texts) and texts_en/own_en_*.txt (2 English texts)
    Written for this project in November 2026 and released with it, under the same terms
    as the rest of the repository. They are original wording, not copied from any source.
    Every person, family, building and place in them is invented; they contain no real
    names, phone numbers, addresses or e-mail addresses.

        own_letter.txt    a letter to a school parents' committee      (~145 words)
        own_story.txt     a short story                                (~200 words)
        own_notice.txt    a notice to the residents of a building      (~100 words)
        own_page.txt      a full page of prose (an essay)              (~380 words)
        own_message.txt   a short message to a parent                  (~100 words)
        own_diary.txt     a personal diary entry                       (~270 words)
        own_moving.txt    a personal account of moving house           (~880 words)
        own_journal.txt   a long personal summer journal               (~2,400 words)
        own_en_note.txt   (English) a short note to a friend           (~130 words)
        own_en_story.txt  (English) a short story                      (~260 words)

texts/wiki_*.txt (5 Hebrew texts)
    Opening paragraphs of Hebrew Wikipedia articles - see WIKIPEDIA-SOURCES.txt for the
    article of each file and its licence (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The Latin toy pack's samples (langpacks/latin_toy/samples/*.txt) were written inside the
project as test material for the toy language.

<mode>/<text>.hz are the golden packages: what the tool must write for each text, byte for
byte. They are made by `python -m tests.golden.make_golden` and listed with their sha256
in manifest.json.
