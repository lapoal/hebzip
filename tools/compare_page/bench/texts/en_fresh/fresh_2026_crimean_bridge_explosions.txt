Beginning on 16 November 2026, as part of the Russo-Ukrainian war, Ukrainian forces have repeatedly targeted the Crimean Bridge with missiles and drones, causing severe damage to the bridge and the destruction of the main span of its road bridge, as well as repeatedly stopping traffic across the bridge.
The first attack, on 16 November 2026, caused one of the road bridge's structural arches to shift by about 15 degrees according to Russian media, forcing the partial closure of road traffic over the bridge. The second attack, on 17 November, collapsed the arch structure and main span of the road bridge below it. Both train and vehicle traffic on the bridge were suspended, with train traffic resuming later that day. A third attack occurred on 19 November, although the extent of damage caused is unknown.
