Grindrod is a South African logistics holding company founded in 1910 and based in Durban, KZN. Listed on the JSE, the company operates through subsidiaries and joint ventures, with activities spanning port and terminal operations, rail freight transport, and associated logistics services.
Grindrod traces its origins to 1910, when Captain John Edward Grindrod established Grindrod & Company, a clearing and forwarding agency in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. The business subsequently expanded into ships' agency services, ship chandling, and coaling. In 1922, Grindrod and Leon Renaud established a coastal shipping enterprise operating between Durban and ports along South Africa's eastern coastline. Renaud subsequently established African Coasters in 1933, which became closely associated with Grindrod's shipping operations.
In 1964, mining company Union Corporation acquired a controlling interest in African Coasters, providing capital for fleet modernization. Two years later, African Coasters merged with Smith's Coasters to form Unicorn Shipping Holdings. The business expanded further through the acquisition of Thesen's Steamship Company in 1967, strengthening its coastal shipping operations.
