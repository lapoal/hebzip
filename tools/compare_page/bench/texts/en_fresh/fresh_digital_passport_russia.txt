Digital passport (Russian: Цифровой паспорт) is a government service in Russia that verifies the identity of a Russian citizen through the use of digital technologies. In official terms, the use of this digital passport service entails "the provision by citizens of the Russian Federation of information contained in documents that verify their identity".
An attempt to introduce a digital passport in the form of an identification card with an embedded chip by 2022 proved unsuccessful, largely due to public distrust of such a format. Beginning in 2023, a digital passport system was introduced that operates remotely, while still maintaining the traditional paper-based document. The initial proposal—which centered on an identification card—would have allowed a citizen to retain a paper document only by formally opting out of using the card.
