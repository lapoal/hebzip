The Murder of Per Häggström was an unsolved homicide committed in Stockholm, Sweden, on 14 October 1996. Häggström, aged 25, was a second-year land surveying student at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. In the early hours of that Monday morning, he was found seriously wounded after being stabbed at Swartlings Riding School on Valhallavägen 99 in the Östermalm district. He was taken to Karolinska Hospital but died during surgery.
Earlier that night, Häggström had attended a student party at the KTH student union building. He left at approximately 04:30, intending to spend the night at a friend's home in Gärdet. His last known sighting was between 04:40 and 04:45 near Stockholm East railway station. The circumstances surrounding the attack remain unclear, although investigators considered robbery a possible motive after discovering that approximately SEK 2,000 was missing from his wallet.
